Syllabus Excellence

Training with airline DNA.

Our structured syllabus is directly aligned with FAA air carrier requirements. Multi-crew cooperation and strict procedural checklists from lesson one significantly reduce transition training time at regional and major carriers.

  • SOP-based multi-engine training from private pilot lessons
  • Crew Resource Management built into the checkride metrics
  • Jet transition bridge programs with glass simulator hours
  • Mentorship led by retired legacy airline captains
