Syllabus Flow

Filed like a flight plan. Flown in phases.

  1. PPLDURATION~55 HRS

    Private Pilot License

    Your foundation. Learn fundamental stick-and-rudder aerodynamic skills, navigation, FAA flight regulations, and execute your first solo flight.

  2. IRDURATION~45 HRS

    Instrument Rating

    Pilot solely by reference to dashboard flight instruments. Master flying in clouds, weather patterns, and advanced ATC radio procedures.

  3. CPLDURATION~120 HRS

    Commercial Single & Multi

    Elite complex maneuvers. Learn advanced standard procedures on our high-performance Piper twin-engine aircraft.

  4. CFIDURATION~25 HRS

    Flight Instructor Certs

    Build invaluable turbine transition hours while earning. Gain your CFI, CFII, and Multi-Engine Instructor (MEI) checkride certificates.
