Core Principles

Our institutional mission

“US Aviation Academy exists to produce career-ready pilots, aviation mechanics, and certified dispatchers through airline-standard safety operations and curriculum at global scale.”

Our Origins

The founding story.

Established in 2004 with a single training airplane, US Aviation Academy has grown into one of the nation's premier training networks.

Today we manage a standardized fleet of over 200 aircraft across multiple state bases, with standard operating procedures directly aligned with global air carrier requirements.

Operational Milestones

Our institutional story.

  1. 2004

    Founded in Denton, TX

    Operations begin with a single training aircraft and regional ground school.

  2. 2008

    FAA Part 141 approval

    First major syllabus expansion, authorizing accelerated pilot pathways.

  3. 2012

    Fleet expands to 50

    Launch of dedicated advanced multi-engine commercial tracks.

  4. 2016

    Multi-campus footprint

    Specialized auxiliary training centers open across multiple states.

  5. 2020

    200+ aircraft milestone

    Recognized as one of the largest standardized glass-cockpit flight schools.

  6. 2024

    Florida launch & degree pathways

    Melbourne base opens alongside the Texas State University transfer articulation.
